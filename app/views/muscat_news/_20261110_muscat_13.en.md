## Welcome in Muscat 13

### General New Features

- New layout for the Template Selection screen to emphasize record structure and material type (handwritten and printed material).
- Names of templates have been adjusted to reflect expected material type and content.
- Colors to show where validations are:
  - **Yellow fields** must be filled out before you save.
  - **Blue fields** will check that related subfields are filled out.
- A banner at the top of a record if the record has comments.

### New in Sources

- Child records can be derived from parent records.
- Validations to support the guidelines and best practices:
  - If Voice/instrument (594$b) is filled out, a Number (594$c) must be filled out.
  - Incipit numbering (031$a, b, c) must be sequential.
  - A validation will emphasize the most important aspects in the field Scoring summaries (240$m).
- Digital Objects support files in Markdown format.
- Two updates for upcoming cataloging developments:
  - Improvements to the templates for inventories (in a test phase).
  - New field, 751, for standardized Geographic Names (in a test phase).

### New in Authorities

- Adjustments when importing from Wikidata, such as for dates and including identifiers from the National Library of the Czech Republic (NKC), descriptive information, and case-insensitive professions.
- Expanded validation for external authority identifiers to match expected format, including ARK normalization.
- The format of dates (100$d, 678$a) must match the guidelines.
- Differentiated and undifferentiated records are clearly marked in the Personal Names search interface.

### Other Improvements

- Fixed several SRU errors and improved import workflows.
- Improved reindexing for new records, with faster parallel processing and clearer reporting of orphaned Solr records.
